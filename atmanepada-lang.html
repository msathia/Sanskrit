आत्मनेपदिधातवः — लङ्लकारः

Topic notes for recognizing and forming past-tense forms of आत्मनेपदी verbs: the initial अ augment, vowel changes, and forms with an उपसर्गः.

लट्लकारः and लङ्लकारः

लट् expresses the present tense. लङ् expresses a past action and adds the augment अ before the verbal base.

लट्लकारः

वर्तते
= it exists / occurs

लङ्लकारः

अ + वर्तत
→ अवर्तत
= it existed / occurred

When the धातुः begins with a vowel

When the लङ् augment अ is placed before a vowel-initial धातुः, the two vowels combine. This is part of forming the verb, rather than sandhi between two separate words.

Vowel-change rule

अ + अ       → आ
अ + इ / ई / ए → ऐ
अ + उ / ऊ     → औ
  • The initial vowel of the धातुः combines with the augment.
  • The resulting आ, ऐ, or औ begins the completed लङ् form.

Worked example: ईक्षते → ऐक्षत

ईक्षते = he/she sees

अ + ईक्षत
अ + ई → ऐ
→ ऐक्षत

ऐक्षत = he/she saw

ऐक्षत is लङ्लकारः, प्रथमपुरुषः, एकवचनम्, आत्मनेपदम्.

Forms with an उपसर्गः

When a verb has a prefix, the लङ् augment follows the prefix. Since वर्तत begins with a consonant, there is no vowel combination in this example.

सम् + वृत्

वर्तते = it exists / occurs
संवर्तते = it arises / comes into being

समवर्तत

सम् + अ + वर्तत
→ समवर्तत
= it arose / came into being

पुरुषसूक्तम् example

शीर्ष्णो द्यौः समवर्तत।

शीर्ष्णो = from the head
द्यौः = heaven / the celestial realm
समवर्तत = arose / came into existence

= From his head, heaven came into existence.

समवर्तत is the आत्मनेपदी verb: लङ्लकारः, प्रथमपुरुषः, एकवचनम्.
