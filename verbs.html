लकाराः — Tenses and Moods

Compare भू (“be / become”) across the लकाराः. The examples are third-person singular (प्रथमपुरुषः, एकवचनम्), using परस्मैपद forms. Tamil uses “அவன்” for consistency; the Sanskrit forms can also refer to she or it.

लकारः · Main idea · Sanskrit example · Tamil meaning
लकारः Main idea Sanskrit example Tamil meaning
लट् Present — is / becomes भवति அவன் இருக்கிறான் / ஆகிறான்.
लङ् Past; traditionally before today अभवत् அவன் இருந்தான் / ஆனான்.
लिट् Past outside direct observation; common in narration बभूव அவன் இருந்தான் / ஆனான் என்று கூறப்படுகிறது.
लुङ् Past action, without the before-today restriction अभूत् அவன் இருந்தான் / ஆனான்.
लृट् General future — will be / become भविष्यति அவன் இருப்பான் / ஆவான்.
लुट् Future; traditionally after today भविता அவன் இனி இருப்பான் / ஆவான்.
लोट् Command, request, or “let…” भवतु அவன் இருக்கட்டும் / ஆகட்டும்.
विधिलिङ् Advice, wish, or possibility — should / may / might भवेत् அவன் இருக்க வேண்டும் / இருக்கக்கூடும் (சூழலுக்கேற்ப).
आशीर्लिङ् Blessing or prayer — may… भूयात् அவன் இருப்பானாக / ஆவானாக!
लृङ् Unfulfilled condition — would / would have अभविष्यत् (நிபந்தனை நிறைவேறியிருந்தால்) அவன் இருந்திருப்பான்.
लेट् Vedic subjunctive — envisaged or wished-for action भवाति அவன் இருக்கலாம் / இருப்பானாக (வேதப் பயன்பாடு; சூழலுக்கேற்ப).

Why eleven rows? The traditional ten include the Vedic-only लेट्. Here लिङ् is shown as two separate uses: विधिलिङ् and आशीर्लिङ्.

The past and future distinctions above are traditional starting points, not rigid translation rules. Tamil glosses are meaning aids, not separate Tamil tense paradigms. लेट् is included for reference; it is not part of ordinary Classical Sanskrit practice.

आत्मनेपद — लोट् and विधिलिङ्

From stating an action to requesting it or expressing what should or might happen.

लट्       लभते
लोट्      लभताम्
विधिलिङ्  लभेत

He obtains → let him obtain → he should/might obtain.

அவன் பெறுகிறான் → அவன் பெறட்டும் → அவன் பெற வேண்டும் / பெறக்கூடும்.

Usage depends on context: लोट् includes polite requests as well as commands. विधिलिङ् can express advice, wishes, or possibility. Their uses overlap; neither has just one English or Tamil equivalent.

The meanings carry over from the reference table, but आत्मनेपद uses its own endings.

Past Forms of आत्मनेपदी Verbs

वर्तते → अवर्तत
ईक्षते → ऐक्षत
संवर्तते → समवर्तत
  • लट् = present; लङ् = past. Add अ before the verbal base, after any उपसर्गः.
  • अ + अ → आ; अ + इ/ई/ए → ऐ; अ + उ/ऊ → औ

क्तवतुप्रत्ययः — Completed actions

प्रत्ययः = suffix · விகுதி. இது க்தவது விகுதி; சந்தி அல்ல.

A past participle formed with क्तवतु describes someone who has completed an action. In these sentences, it expresses “went / has gone.”

रामः ग्रामं गतवान्।

Rama went to the village.

வடிவத்தைப் புரிந்துகொள்ள: இராமன் கிராமத்துக்குச் சென்றவன்.
இயல்பான பொருள்: இராமன் கிராமத்துக்குச் சென்றான்.

सीता ग्रामं गतवती।

Sita went to the village.

வடிவத்தைப் புரிந்துகொள்ள: சீதை கிராமத்துக்குச் சென்றவள்.
இயல்பான பொருள்: சீதை கிராமத்துக்குச் சென்றாள்.

गम् → गतवान् / गतवती: masculine / feminine nominative singular forms.

நினைவுக்குறிப்பு: गतवान् → சென்றவன்; गतवती → சென்றவள். “சென்றவனாவான்” என்று புரிந்துகொண்டாலும், இங்கு “ஆவான்” எதிர்காலத்தைக் குறிக்கவில்லை; செயல் ஏற்கெனவே முடிந்துவிட்டது.

Participles From लट्लकारः

गायति → गायन् (शतृ)
वन्दते → वन्दमानः (शानच्)
रामः गायन् पचति।
  • “Rama cooks while singing”: a participle describes the person doing an action.
  • परस्मैपद → शतृ; आत्मनेपद → शानच् in the present-participle patterns studied here.
  • Forms change with gender, number, and case: धरन्ती, धरन्तः, आरभमाणस्य.

लभ् — Full Conjugation Charts

लभ् means “obtain / receive” (பெறு). Each chart shows all nine कर्तरि आत्मनेपद forms. The row order is उत्तमपुरुषः, मध्यमपुरुषः, प्रथमपुरुषः.

How to read the charts

Each chart has nine forms: three persons × three numbers. Columns: singular (one), dual (two), and plural (three or more).

  • उत्तमपुरुषः — I / we · நான் / நாம்
  • मध्यमपुरुषः — You · நீ / நீங்கள்
  • प्रथमपुरुषः — He, she, it / they · அவன், அவள், அது / அவர்கள், அவை

Each Sanskrit form has an English sentence meaning. “He” also stands for “she” or “it”; the verb form is the same. Tamil summaries below the charts are additional study aids. The exact sense depends on context.

Ten lakāras, ten study charts: The traditional ten लकाराः include Vedic लेट् and count लिङ् once. These charts omit लेट् and show लिङ् as two charts: विधिलिङ् and आशीर्लिङ्.

Forms: Samsādhanī, लभ्, कर्तरि paradigms.

लोट् — Commands and requests

लभ् · लोट् · आत्मनेपदम् · कर्तरि
पुरुषःPersonएकवचनम्Singularद्विवचनम्Dualबहुवचनम्Plural
उत्तमपुरुषःलभैLet me receive.लभावहैLet us two receive.लभामहैLet us receive.
मध्यमपुरुषःलभस्वPlease receive.लभेथाम्Please receive, both of you.लभध्वम्Please receive, all of you.
प्रथमपुरुषःलभताम्Let him receive.लभेताम्Let them two receive.लभन्ताम्Let them receive.

அவன் பெறட்டும்.

लोट् also covers polite requests. उत्तमपुरुष forms have the sense “let me / let us.”

लोट् in the Bhagavad Gītā — Arise and gain glory

तस्मात्त्वमुत्तिष्ठ यशो लभस्व

Therefore, arise and gain glory.

Bhagavad Gītā 11.33, opening line — Krishna addresses Arjuna, urging him to rise and act.

तस्मात् + त्वम् + उत्तिष्ठ + यशः + लभस्व

WordMeaning
तस्मात्Therefore
त्वम्You
उत्तिष्ठArise! / Stand up!
यशःGlory / fame / honour
लभस्वObtain! / Gain!

लभ् → लभस्व: लोट्, मध्यमपुरुषः, एकवचनम्, आत्मनेपदम् — “You, obtain!” Here the object is यशः: “Gain glory!”

उत् + स्था → उत्तिष्ठ: also लोट्, मध्यमपुरुषः, एकवचनम्, but परस्मैपदम् — “Arise!” Both verbs directly address one person.

Sandhi: तस्मात् + त्वम् → तस्मात्त्वम्; त्वम् + उत्तिष्ठ → त्वमुत्तिष्ठ; यशः + लभस्व → यशो लभस्व.

लेट् — Vedic reference

लेट् is Vedic-only. An attested लभ् paradigm has not been verified here, so no speculative chart is supplied. The ten charts above treat विधिलिङ् and आशीर्लिङ् separately.
